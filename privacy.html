🦖 WTF Boss Manager

What The Fark — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Operator: Wicked Astrid — What The Fark

1. Introduction

WTF Boss Manager is a Discord application created to organize ARK: Survival Ascended boss fights for the What The Fark community. This policy explains how the application handles information.

2. Information Collected

The application may process or store Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, boss fight schedules, event details, signup records, waitlist positions and timestamps.

The starter application does not request passwords, payment information, email addresses or private Discord messages.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to create and manage boss fights, reserve seats, maintain waitlists, display rosters, send reminders and support authorized event management.

4. Data Storage and Security

The starter application stores event and signup information in a SQLite database in its hosting environment. Access to application credentials and stored records should be limited to authorized administrators. No storage method is completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

Participant Discord mentions and event rosters may be visible to members with access to the relevant Discord channels. The application does not intentionally sell personal information or share stored data with advertisers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Event and signup records may remain stored after events end or are cancelled. Users may request deletion of their stored information by contacting the operator. Deletion may require manual administrator action.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses Discord's platform and API and may rely on a third-party hosting provider. These services have their own privacy practices.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data handling changes.

9. Contact and Data Requests

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact Wicked Astrid through the What The Fark Discord community.
